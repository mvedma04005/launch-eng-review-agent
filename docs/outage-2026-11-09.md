## What it adds up to

- Availability equals one person's laptop being open and on a good network.
- Throughput equals one review at a time. Thirty PRs means two hours.
- Every error is terminal. Network blips, restarts and storage hiccups all end in "run `agent.py retry <id>`", which only Justin can do.
- The operator cannot use the tool on PRs they have touched, because they are the tool's identity.

The rebuild's answers, in the same order: a GitHub App identity on a host that is not a laptop; a leased queue with many workers; transient errors requeue with backoff and crashed leases recover automatically; the bot is not a person, so no identity collision.

Note: the deployed agent is ahead of the public repository I reviewed. It posts queue position and ETA, tags findings as "Rokt rule", "Orbit rule", "One-way door" and "split this PR", runs comment-only, and links a log viewer at `le-review-queue.rokt.chatgpt.site`. Those are good ideas and are easy to carry into policy packs as rule provenance tags.
